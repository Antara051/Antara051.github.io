01 / ABOUT

Engineering for the next frontier.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Space Systems Engineering at Kyushu Institute of Technology (Kyutech), Japan, and a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University, Bangladesh.

My research centers on lightweight terrain perception and autonomous lunar rover traversability assessment, with a broader interest in planetary exploration and embedded intelligent systems.

My experience spans small satellite development, assembly, integration and testing, embedded systems, ground station software, and international space education initiatives.

MISSION OPERATIONS
◉
GROUND SEGMENT DEVELOPMENT

VERTECS Ground Station Software

Led the development and validation of ground station software for VERTECS (Visible Extragalactic Background RadiaTion Exploration by CubeSat), a 6U astronomical nanosatellite mission dedicated to studying extragalactic background light and understanding the evolution of the universe. Developed an integrated platform enabling spacecraft commanding, real-time telemetry reception, satellite health monitoring, and mission operations across Kyutech and JAXA ground stations, contributing to mission readiness and reliable ground-to-space communication.

Ground Station Software · Mission Systems · Operations
