01 / INTRODUCTION

About Me

Exploring the intersection of space systems, intelligent perception, and planetary exploration.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Space Systems Engineering at Kyushu Institute of Technology (Kyutech), Japan, and a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University, Bangladesh.

My experience spans small satellite development, assembly, integration and testing, embedded systems, ground station operations, and research in perception for planetary surface exploration.
